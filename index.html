02

Where are my cards stored?

Right here in your browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to a server – not even to the website hosting this page. That’s private and needs no account. It also means:

  • Another browser or another device starts empty.
  • Clearing your browsing data deletes your cards.
  • If you open the file straight from your computer (not via a web address) and then move or rename it, some browsers won’t find your cards anymore. Move it back and they reappear.

So make a backup now and then: ··· → Export as JSON saves everything into one file. Import JSON brings it back – in any browser, on any device.

03

Which browser?

Every modern browser works. They differ in how long they keep your cards and whether you can install the page as an app.

Recommended

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc

Keep your cards reliably, work offline and can install the page as an app on your computer.

Good

Firefox

Keeps your cards reliably and works offline. Installing as an app is limited. It may ask once if the page may keep its data – say yes.

With care

Safari (Mac, iPhone, iPad)

Works fine, but deletes your cards if you don’t open the page for about 7 days. Installing it as an app avoids that – or keep a backup.

04

Install it as an app?

When the page runs on a website (https), you can add it to your desktop, Dock or home screen. It then opens in its own window with its own icon, like a small program. That’s handy on a computer too: one click from the Dock or taskbar, no browser tab to hunt for, and it sits neatly next to your editor, chat or game.

Chrome / Edge
Install icon in the address bar, or menu → Install …
Safari on Mac
File → Add to Dock
iPhone / iPad
Share → Add to Home Screen
Pros
  • Own window and icon
  • Opens without internet
  • Safari: cards no longer vanish after 7 days
Cons
  • Safari: the app keeps its own cards, separate from the browser – move them with Export/Import
  • Page updates arrive the next time you’re online
  • Only for the website version, not for a file opened from your computer
05

No internet?

Once you’ve opened the website version, it also loads without internet. Your changes are saved on your device either way. Opened as a file from your computer, it never needs internet at all.

06

Private / incognito windows

The page works in a private window too, but that window has its own, temporary memory:

  • Your usual cards don’t show up there – it starts with the starter content.
  • Whatever you add is kept while the window is open, even if you reload.
  • When you close the last private window, everything in it is gone for good.

Great for trying things out. If you create something worth keeping, export it before closing the window.
